01Why it exists

The visibilityproblem.

Exceptional dogs may remain obscure, while familiar names vary in the depth of proof they offer. Pedigrees, clinical findings and lineage histories are dispersed across websites, social feeds, messages, paper files and private archives. Doberman Index brings that material into a coherent public reference. Substantiation should lead attention.

02The problem

When evidenceis scattered.

Ancestry becomes harder to trace when clinical documents, results and family links live apart. Incompatible formats slow evaluation; personal familiarity stands in for clarity. A permanent DI number and explicit connections reveal the complete picture.

03

03Definition

A registry.Built for clarity.

It is an editorially controlled registry for canine subjects, breeder programs, progeny histories and bloodlines. Every digital dossier gathers identifiers, documentation and lineage; interpretation remains with the reader. Its purpose is simple: make complex breed knowledge intelligible, traceable and useful.

01

Consistent dossiers

Pedigree, health indicators, temperament, performance, reproduction and media follow a shared data model. Empty fields stay explicit.

02

Searchable presence

Each publication receives a permanent address, accessible by name, profile ID or direct ancestry link.

03

Persistent identifiers connect parents, breeder portfolios and related profiles as the genealogy expands.
